# 🛡️ Chakravyuh: Chaos & Control

> **Hackathon Submission for Problem Statement 02: Chaos Control**  
> An immersive, high-speed asymmetric multiplayer & single-player strategic arcade game set in a royal Indian cyber-mythic universe.

---

## ✨ Overview
**Chakravyuh** is a competitive asymmetric game where one player acts as the agile **Indian Warrior (Runner)** attempting to collect sacred relics, while the opponent plays as the multi-armed **Rakshasa (Saboteur)** utilizing environmental shrine abilities to corrupt paths and disrupt their success. 

Engineered for blazing-fast 60FPS browser performance and cross-platform native desktop execution.

---

## 🎮 Core Features & Mechanics
* **Asymmetric Roles & Automatic Role Swapping**: Play seamlessly swaps controls and roles between the Runner and the Saboteur after every round.
* **Balanced 5-Second Shrine Abilities**: 
  * 🔥 **Agni**: Ricocheting fireballs.
  * 🌀 **Maayu**: Inverts controls and warps the Runner.
  * ⛰️ **Kala**: Rises stone pillars to block shortcuts.
* **Dual Multiplayer Modes**: Live online custom room codes (powered by Firebase) across two separate laptops, or local split-keyboard.
* **Single-Player vs. AI Bots**: Play against 3 intelligent AI tiers (Easy, Medium, Hard).

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Game Engine**: Phaser 3
* **Backend & Database**: Firebase Authentication, Realtime Database, Firestore
* **Desktop Wrapper**: Electron
